# Listening and Assertive Communication

## Question 1
## Steps to do Active listening

### Avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts
### Focus on the speaker and try to understand
### Try not to interrupt the other person; let them finish and then respond
### Use door openers—they show you are interested and keep the other person talking
### Show that you are listening through your body language
### If appropriate, take notes

## Question 2
## According to Fisher's model, the key points of reflective listening

### Listen carefully — Pay full attention to what the other person is saying.
### Understand their meaning — Try to understand both their words and feelings.
### Repeat in your own words — Explain what you understood using simple words.
### Check your understanding — Make sure you understood the person correctly.
### Do not judge or interrupt — Give the person time to express their thoughts.
### Show empathy — Try to understand the situation from the other person's point of view.
### Ask questions if needed — Clarify anything that is unclear instead of making assumptions.
