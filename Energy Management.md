## Question 6

* Exercise changes brain anatomy, physiology, and function.
* Exercise actually produces brand-new brain cells.
* Exercise improves long-term memory.
* Exercise improves attention function in the prefrontal cortex.
* We do not get an immediate effect on our mood from exercise, but the effects last for a long time.

## Question 7

* Do regular exercise without skipping a day.
* Do easy exercises that we can easily perform.
* Increase the intensity or do some tough exercises multiple times a week. This makes our body handle challenging tasks so that it can also handle tough situations.
