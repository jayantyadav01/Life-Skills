# Learning Process

## Feynman Technique

Don't leave practicing any topic until you are able to explain it in your own words.

## Interesting Story

The most interesting idea to learn a new topic is to take your notes and just glance at a page once, and then think about or memorize the thought or idea written on that page. If you are not able to come up with an explanation, then go back to the source and again grasp everything. That's all we need to learn faster any new skill.

## Active and Diffuse Mode of Thinking

### Active Mode of Thinking

Focus or concentrate on any specific topic or idea and solve problems or memorize any fact.

At this time, we are in active mode.

e.g., learning a new topic or memorizing any fact or thought.

### Diffused Mode of Thinking

Diffused mode of thinking is like relating any past data or fact with other data. It happens when you have a relaxed mind and give enough time to think about any unrelated history, and sometimes an unreal or unpredictable thought comes up in the mind.

## Just 20 Hours to Learn Anything

* De-construct the skill
* Learn enough to self-correct
* Remove practice barriers
* Practice for at least 20 hours
