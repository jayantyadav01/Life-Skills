Tiny Habits & Atomic Habits – Questions and Answers

Question 1

The most interesting idea for me was that a habit does not have to start with a big action. BJ Fogg explains that we can start with a very small behavior that is easy to complete. For example, instead of deciding to exercise for one hour, we can start with just two push-ups. I found this interesting because small actions feel easier and reduce the pressure of starting something new. The idea of celebrating even a very small success also stood out to me because it can make us feel successful and encourage us to continue.

Question 2

B = MAP means Behavior = Motivation + Ability + Prompt.

M – Motivation: The desire or willingness to do the behavior.

A – Ability: How easy or difficult the behavior is to perform.

P – Prompt: A reminder or trigger that tells us to perform the behavior.

Question 3

"Shine" or celebrating after completing a habit is important because it creates a positive feeling immediately after the behavior. That positive emotion helps reinforce the behavior and makes us more likely to repeat it.

Question 4

The most interesting idea for me was the idea of getting 1% better every day. A small improvement may not look important on a single day, but repeated improvements can create a big difference over a long period of time.

Question 5

The book's perspective is that changing our identity is more powerful than focusing only on the result we want.

Instead of thinking, "I want to read more books," I can think, "I am a reader." Instead of saying, "I want to become a good programmer," I can think, "I am a programmer who practices every day."
